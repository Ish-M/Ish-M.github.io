Career Goals

Long-term vision

My long-term goal is to design the chips that power the next generation of computing, especially processors and accelerators for machine learning. My research showed me how often models are limited by the memory and compute beneath them, and my RISC-V and chip-layout work has shown me how much I enjoy solving those problems in silicon.

After graduating, I plan to work as a digital design or computer architecture engineer at a semiconductor company, and I'm keeping the door open to a master's degree or PhD in computer architecture or VLSI. Further out, co-founding Medi-Loc has me interested in eventually leading hardware teams and bringing new products from idea to market, either as an entrepreneur or as part of a larger company.

Where I'm headed

Target roles: Digital design engineer, computer architecture engineer

Focus areas: Processors and ML accelerators, VLSI, hardware verification

Graduate study: M.S. or PhD in computer architecture or VLSI

Long term: Leading hardware teams and bringing new products to market
