Publications

Estimation of biological aging based on T-cell differentiation trajectories: emerging and future avenues

Frontiers in Aging, vol. 6 · 2025 · Shared first author

Bhasin, A.*, Marwaha, I.K.*, & Nooka, L.S.* (2025). (* shared first authorship)

I co-led this peer-reviewed study and served as corresponding author. We investigated T-cell differentiation trajectories as quantifiable biomarkers of biological age and proposed the naive-to-exhausted T-cell ratio as a new metric for assessing immune aging.
